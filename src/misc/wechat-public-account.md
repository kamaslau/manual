# WeChat Public Account Opening Manifest

WeChat Public Account (abbr. WPA) is a useful branding/operating approach available on the largest social communication software offered by Tencent.

## Types

There are 2 types of WPA: subscription account and service account. The official explaination of the differences between them are as instructed on [https://mp.weixin.qq.com/](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/)

## Account Opening

To establish a WPA account, the following items are required.

_For photos, images should be provided in jpg(jpeg)/png format less then 500kb in size and all 4 corners should be revealed for document or card._

- [ ] An email account as auth code recipient‌
- [ ] \[Deferable\] An Avatar image in square shape
- [ ] Photo of ID document of the enterprise law person
- [x] Photo of ID document of one Chinese citizen as operator/liaison, if law person is not Chinese (political, not ethnical)
- [ ] Mobile number of law person
- [x] Mobile number of the operator/liaison
- Any other additional materials asked by Tencent

Identity verification is required to unlock WPA features, which asks for:

- [ ] Photo of ID document, varies on account subject identity
  - For enterprise: register license
  - For individual: ID card for Chinese citizen or passport for foreign citizen
- Bank account verification for enterprise
  - [ ] Corporate bank account: a tiny amount transfer will be made by Tencent to verify the identity of the subject of a WPA
    - Bank name
    - Account name
  - \[Deferred\] The amount of the described transfer (the logic is that Tencent believes the bank account supervisor could reasonably represent the corresponding enterprise on non-transaction events like social media account opening up, like WPA)
- Any other additional materials asked by Tencent
